# Allonssy Short-Form Video Marketing Strategy & Scripts
## (TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts)

---

## 🌟 Overview & Distribution Strategy
This repository contains an automated video rendering engine (`marketing/auto_video_generator.py`) and daily dispatcher (`marketing/daily_email_dispatcher.py`) powered by **GitHub Actions**.

- **Format:** 1080x1920 (9:16 vertical video)
- **Voiceover:** Ultra-realistic Microsoft Neural TTS (`en-US-ChristopherNeural`)
- **Visuals:** Dynamic Ken Burns camera pan, Allonssy branding watermark, smartphone mockup showcase, and attention-grabbing hook typography.
- **Channels:** **Instagram Reels**, **TikTok**, and **YouTube Shorts**.
- **Delivery:** Automatically delivered daily via email with 1-tap download link from GitHub Pages and pre-filled Reddit / community post copy.
